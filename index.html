Riyadh Metro Analysis
Interactive Power BI dashboard & map analyzing Riyadh Metro routes, stations, and station types.

Goal: Analyze the Riyadh Metro network through an interactive Power BI dashboard and map, covering routes, stations, station types, and interchange stations.

Key insights
  • The network has 6 routes and 83 distinct stations (94 route stops, since interchange stations count once per route); the Blue (25) and Orange (22) routes hold half of all route stops.
  • Elevated stations are the most common type (40 of 94 route stops, ~43%), followed by deep underground (38); the Yellow route is the shortest (9 stops) and ends at the airport terminals.
  • Ten stations are interchanges: the Financial Center connects 3 routes, and nine others (including stc and Al-Hamra) connect 2 routes each.

Goal: Analyze job postings from the public, private, and semi-government sectors on the Jadarah platform in 2025 to understand demand by region and job title.

Python & Forecasting
McDonald's Stock Data Analysis
Python analysis and stock price forecasting using Meta's Prophet model for time series prediction.

Goal: Analyze McDonald's (MCD) daily stock data from 2000 to 2026 to understand price trends and risk, measure the impact of the 2008 crisis and COVID-19, and forecast future trends with Prophet.

Key insights
  • Strong long-term growth: $1 invested in 2000 grew to nearly 15× by 2026, as the closing price rose from $6.77 (Mar 2003) to $319.65 (Dec 2025).
  • Resilient through crises: daily volatility fell after 2008 (0.0176 → 0.0126) and barely changed after COVID-19 (0.0143 → 0.0142), even though March 2020 produced both the best day (+18.13%) and worst day (−15.88%).
  • Forecast: the Prophet model projects the upward trend continuing about five years beyond January 2026, with a widening confidence interval showing that long-term predictions capture the trend rather than exact prices.
